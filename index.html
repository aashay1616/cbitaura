The fest

Not a friendly.
A national stage.

AURA is CBIT’s flagship inter-engineering sports meet — certified officials, serious brackets, and a campus that runs on three days of pure competition. This is where colleges travel to prove something.

01 · Conduct

Run like a real tournament

End-to-end by Chaitanya Kreeda & Physical Education. Certified referees. League-cum-knockout energy.

02 · Scale

1,500+ athletes

Last edition already proved the footprint — 10+ sports, 40–50 colleges, ₹3L+ prizes.

03 · Stage

CBIT · Gandipet

NAAC A+ · autonomous · grounds, courts, and indoor halls built for national play.
