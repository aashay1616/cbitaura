Registration desk

When a team registers and pays, you get an email alert (live mode). Open this desk, open their payment screenshot, then Verify (captain gets confirmation) or Reject.

How approvals work

  1. Team submits on register form (sport, details, payment screenshot + UTR).
  2. You are notified by email: “New registration · pending” + college, sport, ref code.
  3. Open this page → filter Pending → open screenshot → check UTR/amount.
  4. Verify + email = approved; captain receives confirmation. Reject = not accepted.

Access URL (bookmark this): https://cbitaura.in/admin.html · not linked from the public nav.
