Registration desk

Teams register, pay, and upload a screenshot. They are asked to message / coordinate with CC after submitting. Open this desk, check their proof, then Verify or Reject (no automatic emails for now — confirm captains on Instagram / WhatsApp).

How approvals work

  1. Team submits on the register form (details + payment screenshot + UTR).
  2. Team should contact you (IG / WhatsApp) with their reference code.
  3. Open this page → filter Pending → View screenshot → check UTR / amount.
  4. Verify = approved in the system. Reject = not accepted (add a reason).
  5. Message the captain manually to confirm approval (email automation not enabled yet).
